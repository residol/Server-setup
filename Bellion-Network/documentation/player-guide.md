# WELCOME TO BELLION CASTLE

## Getting Started Tutorial
- Join the server at `play.bellion.it`
- Explore the medieval hub castle
- Use NPCs or /menu to select a gamemode

## Gamemode Guides Complete
- BedWars: Destroy beds, eliminate teams, upgrade gear
- SkyWars: Loot chests, survive on floating islands
- KitPvP: Choose kits, fight in the arena, earn rewards
- BuildBattle: Build to the theme, vote, win prizes
- Arena1v1: Queue for duels, climb the ELO ladder
- Vanilla: Survive, build, claim land, trade
- PrisonSkyblock: Mine, rank up, expand your island

## Rules & Guidelines
- No cheating, hacking, or exploiting
- Respect staff and players
- No griefing or stealing
- Keep chat friendly and appropriate

## FAQ Section
- Q: How do I get coins? A: Play games, win events, vote
- Q: How do I join a gamemode? A: Use NPCs or /menu
- Q: How do I claim land? A: Use a stick in Vanilla

## Economy Guide
- Earn Bellion Coins by playing
- Spend coins in /shop for upgrades, cosmetics, and more
- Castle Gems are premium, buyable in the store
- Honor Points from PvP, Build Tokens from BuildBattle

## Ranking System Explained
- Progress from Guest to King by playing and earning XP
- VIP ranks available in the store
- Staff ranks are assigned by admins
